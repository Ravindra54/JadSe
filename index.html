The real problem

It's probably not your shampoo.

Most hair-fall products focus on ingredients. Ours focuses on the one thing dermatologists actually flag for Bangalore residents: water hardness.

01

Bangalore's water is hard

Borewell water across most Bangalore neighbourhoods carries high mineral (calcium & magnesium) content, which coats hair strands and dries out the scalp over time.

02

Dry scalp weakens roots

A dehydrated scalp becomes tight and flaky, which can make hair feel more brittle and prone to breakage and shedding.

03

Herb-boiled water helps

Curry leaf, onion, rosemary, clove and fenugreek are simmered into the water itself — a light mist you spray between washes, with nothing to rinse out.

Routine

Two minutes, twice a day.

Step 1

Shake gently

Give the bottle a light shake before every use to keep the mineral balance even.

Step 2

Spray on dry scalp

Part hair into sections and mist directly onto a dry scalp, roughly 8–10 sprays.

Step 3

Massage in

Use fingertips to gently massage the scalp for 1–2 minutes to help absorption.

Step 4

Rinse normally

If you leave it on for a while, hair may feel a little hard — just rinse it off normally with water when you're done.
